Assistant Professor at the [Centre of Studies in Resources Engineering (CSRE)](https://www.csre.iitb.ac.in/), IIT Bombay. His research focuses on satellite remote sensing of water bodies, hydrological modelling of regulated rivers, and the development of open-source tools for global water-resource monitoring. He completed his PhD and MS in Civil & Environmental Engineering from the University of Washington, Seattle, and his MTech from IIT Roorkee.
