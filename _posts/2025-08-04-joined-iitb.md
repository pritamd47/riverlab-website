We are officially launching RIVER Lab at the Centre of Studies in Resources Engineering (CSRE), IIT Bombay. The lab will focus on satellite remote sensing of water bodies, regulated river hydrology, and open-source hydrological software. Positions for PhD students and research associates are open – see the [Join](../join) page for details.
