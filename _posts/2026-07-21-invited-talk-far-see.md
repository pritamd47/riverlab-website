The PI presented an invited talk (online) on "AI driven streamflow prediction in Regulated Basins" at the FAR-SEE 2026 event on AI/ML applications in physical sciences at the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL). The talk focused on the performance of LSTM models in streamflow estimation with a special focus on regulated basins. Mr. Maitra Tiwari, who was a summer intern in 2026 played a major role in developing the LSTM systems and running various experiments for comparison of training and evaluation on unregulated vs. regulated systems.
