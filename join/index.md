## What We Look For

A strong application will typically include:

- Background in one or more of: remote sensing, civil/environmental engineering, geosciences, computer science, or a related field
- Familiarity with Python or another scientific programming language
- Interest in satellite remote sensing, hydrology, or water-resource management
- For PhD: demonstrable ability to write clearly

Prior experience with satellite remote sensing, geospatial data analysis and hydrological models is a plus but not required.

{% include section.html %}

## How to Apply

Send an email to **pritam.das [at] iitb.ac.in** with the subject line **"Prospective [PhD/RA/Intern]"** and include:

1. Your CV (PDF)
2. A short statement (≤ 300 words) describing your research interests and why you want to join the lab
3. Unofficial transcripts
4. For PhD applicants: one writing sample (thesis chapter, report, or published paper)
